About the course

A syllabus older than the compass

Long before satellites, sailors and shepherds alike could tell direction, time, and season from the sky alone. Wayfinder teaches that same practical literacy — slowly, outdoors, one clear night at a time.

Each week pairs a short live session with a take-home task: step outside, find a specific star, and use it. By the end of six weeks, you'll be able to find north, tell time after dark, and estimate your latitude — all without a single instrument.

"You don't memorize the sky. You learn to ask it questions."

— Course philosophy, Week 1 session
