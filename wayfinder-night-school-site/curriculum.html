Curriculum

Six stars, one course

Each week adds one point to the map. By the final session, you'll have drawn the whole course yourself — on paper and in the sky.

I

Week OneFinding North

Circumpolar stars, Polaris, and how to orient yourself on any clear night without a compass.

II

Week TwoReading the Ecliptic

The path the sun, moon, and planets share across the sky, and why it's the key to everything that follows.

III

Week ThreeThe Almanac Sky

How star positions shift through the night, the season, and your latitude — and how to predict them.

IV

Week FourLunar Navigation

Reading moon phase and position to estimate the time and hold a bearing after the stars are washed out.

V

Week FiveLatitude by Starlight

A single star sighting, a bit of arithmetic, and your latitude — the same method sailors trusted for centuries.

VI

Week SixThe Open Passage

A practical night out: plot a short route using only what you've learned, sky and all.
